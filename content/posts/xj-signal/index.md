## Symptoms

On my 1989 Jeep Cherokee XJ, the right turn signal indicator on the dashboard would light up whenever I turned on the headlights or parking lights.

The right turn signal also would not blink normally. Instead, it stayed solid when activated.

Common symptoms include:

- Turn signal indicator glowing dimly
- Signal stays solid instead of flashing
- Turn signal comes on with headlights
- Fast blinking or no blinking
- One side acting weird with parking lights

## The Fix

### Basics

Obviously, the first step is to check that the bulb isn't just burnt out. You can do this by removing the bulb and testing it with a known good bulb. You can also do this by examining the filament inside carefully to ensure it is entact. It can also help if you have a multimeter with continuity. Set the meter to resistance/continuity and probe the bulb's contacts. You should see a number appear, and the meter will beep if you are using continuity mode. A burnt out bulb will show no connection (usually indicated with "OL" on the multimeter screen).

Also, ensure that the fuse is not blown. This is an easy check to make, and will save you a lot of time and frustration.

### Grounding

The first thing I did was to identify the grounds used for the turn signals. On the 1989 model XJ, there is one grounding point for the headlights and the turning signals. This is located on the drivers side of the vehicle, next to the washer fluid reservoir.

I disconnected the bolt holding the wires to the body and cleaned both the eyelets and the body of the vehicle with sandpaper until they were shining. This is a common point of failure on these vehicles, and cleaning it up often solves the problem for most people. In my case, however, it did not.
