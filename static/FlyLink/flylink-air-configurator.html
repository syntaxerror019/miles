Welcome 👋

  1. Plug your FlyLink Sim into your computer.
  2. Click Connect and pick it in the popup (it may be called Arduino Leonardo or FlyLink Sim).
  3. Follow the steps. Changes work instantly, and one click saves them.

1 · Wiggle your sticks

Switch your radio on and move each stick. The bar should follow the stick. If one goes the wrong way, tick Reverse.

2 · Calibrate

This teaches your FlyLink Sim how far your sticks really move, so full stick means full stick in your sim.

3 · Check your switches

Flip your switches. A dot should light up for each one that's on.

4 · Save

Done! Launch your sim and calibrate the new controller there too.

Sticks & axes

FlyLink Sim shows up as a joystick with 8 axes. Choose which radio channel drives each one. Min / Center / Max are the pulse widths your radio sends (calibration fills these in). Deadband ignores tiny movement around centre. Expo softens the middle (positive) or sharpens it (negative). If signal lost is what the sim sees when the radio disconnects (-32767 to 32767, throttle should be -32767).

Switches → buttons

A button presses when its channel passes a level (1500 is the middle). For a 3-position switch: Above 1750 = top, Below 1250 = bottom, Middle 250 = centre. Toggle makes a push-style switch behave like on/off.
